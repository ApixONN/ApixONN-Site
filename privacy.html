ApixONN & Dalil

Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. It covers the ApixONN ERP and Dalil, our AI assistant, including when Dalil is used over WhatsApp.

1Who we are

ApixONN is a software company based in Qatar. For any privacy question, contact us at info@apixonn.com.

2What we collect

  • Account details — your name and email.
  • Documents you upload — the files you give Dalil to answer from.
  • Questions and answers — the questions asked and the answers given.
  • On WhatsApp — your phone number, WhatsApp profile name, and the content of your messages.

3Why we collect it

Only to provide the service — answering questions from your own documents. We do not use your documents to train public AI models.

4Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the providers needed to run the service: Meta, to deliver WhatsApp messages; our hosting provider; and AI model providers where they are used to generate an answer.

5Storage and security

Your data is stored securely, and access is limited to authorised people who need it to run the service.

6Your rights

You can ask to see or delete your data at any time by emailing info@apixonn.com. WhatsApp users can also send "unlink" at any time to stop and disconnect.
