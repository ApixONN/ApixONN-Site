The ApixONN ERP

One system for the whole business

Finance, people, projects and assets in one connected platform — with approvals, reporting and an employee portal built in. No more a dozen disconnected spreadsheets.

Finance & Accounting

Invoices, receipts, journals, chart of accounts, bills, POs, bank reconciliation, budgets and tax — real double-entry GL.

HR & Payroll

Employees, contracts, grades, leaves, attendance and org chart — plus a full payroll engine: cycles, payslips, loans, tax, bank files.

Projects & Resources

Phases and milestones, risks, budgets, earned-value analytics, allocations, timesheets and capacity planning.

Inventory

Products and suppliers, goods received (GRN), goods issued and live stock — every movement posted to the ledger.

Property

Portfolios and units, leases, deposits and cheques, maintenance, and owner statements & settlements.

Clients & Sales

Clients, an opportunity pipeline and statements of work — the commercial hub every project and invoice hangs off.

Workflow & Approvals

Multi-step approvals with separation of duties across the whole system — nothing big moves on one person's say-so.

Reports & Analytics

Financial statements, HR, payroll, projects and resource analytics — the numbers, always reconciled to the ledger.

Employee Web Portal Every employee gets self-service: timesheets, leave and HR requests, tasks, documents and their profile.
